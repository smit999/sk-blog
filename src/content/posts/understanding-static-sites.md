In a world of complex web applications, there's a certain beauty in the simplicity of a static site.

### Key Benefits

* **Speed:** Pages are pre-built, so they load instantly.
* **Security:** With no database or server-side processing, the attack surface is minimal.
* **Scalability:** Static files can be served from a CDN for incredible performance under load.

Tools like Astro, Hugo, and Jekyll have made building static sites easier than ever.
